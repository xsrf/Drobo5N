# Disassembly
Disassembly instructions for the Drobo 5N

## Prerequisites
- PH1 screwdriver ⊕
- PH2 screwdriver ⊕
- ~3mm flat hat screwdriver ⊖

## Instructions in shot text form
1. Remove magnetic front cover
1. Remove drives
1. Unhook door from bottom opening
1. Unscrew bottom feet (4x PH2)
1. Lift the outer shell slightly at the half with the opening and slide the inner parts out to the back end (pay attention to the plastic catch, it remains part of the housing)
1. The front rubber seal around the drive cage might have got loose now, remove it
1. Unclip the back plastic (2 clips on each side, one on the top)
1. Unclip the front plastic (2 clips on each side, one on the top)
1. Remove the top and bottom screw on each side (4x PH1)
1. Slide the top of the case backwards (against the sides) for ~3-5mm and then turn the case around on its top
1. Try to pry the bottom part of the back (where the connector openings are) backwards. A flat hat screwdriver might help to lever against the chamfered edges
1. Turn it back right way up
1. Slide tha top/back all the way back until the back side is free - then lift it carefully ~10mm. Be aware of the Power switch and Fan cables inside!
1. Carefully unplug the power switch from the right side, then unplug the Fan (can be done from the left side)
1. Remove the top/back
1. optionally: Use a flat hat screw driver or a dull pin to push out the plastic pegs, that hold the Fan, from the back side. Then unclip the Fan cable clips and remove the Fan
1. optionally: Remove the Power switch by pushing it from the back while carefully compressing the flaps on the side with a flat hat screwdriver
1. Unplug the battery
1. Remove the remaining two screws on each side (4x PH1)
1. Carefully lift the backplane assembly up
1. Unscrew the backplane from its frame (8x PH2)
1. Unscrew battery holder from its frame (2x PH2)
1. Unscrew the main PCB and its shield (6x PH1)
1. Carefully lift the PCB shield without bending its fingers on the side
1. Remove the main PCB
1. optionally: Unscrew the USB DOM (Disk on module) and lift it (1x PH1)
